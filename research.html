Guides & Studies

Peptide background guides and published studies, summarised from Peptide Health Digest and shared with the site owner's permission. Each entry links to its source page. For laboratory research reference only — nothing here is medical advice, a dosing protocol or a recommendation of use.

Published studies

Citations are to the original publications. Animal and cell-culture (preclinical) studies are labelled as such and do not establish effects in humans.

Human clinical trial

Semaglutide and Cardiovascular Outcomes in Obesity without Diabetes (SELECT)

Cardiovascular-outcomes trial of once-weekly semaglutide in adults with obesity and established cardiovascular disease, without diabetes (17,604 participants).

Lincoff AM, et al. New England Journal of Medicine, 2023-12-14. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2307563
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Human clinical trial

Once-Weekly Semaglutide in Adults with Overweight or Obesity (STEP 1)

Placebo-controlled trial of once-weekly semaglutide in adults with overweight or obesity, with body-weight change at 68 weeks as an endpoint (1,961 participants).

Wilding JPH, et al. New England Journal of Medicine, 2021-03-18. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2032183
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Human clinical trial

Tirzepatide Once Weekly for the Treatment of Obesity (SURMOUNT-1)

Multinational phase 3 trial of the dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor co-agonist tirzepatide in adults with obesity (2,539 participants).

Jastreboff AM, et al. New England Journal of Medicine, 2022-07-21. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2206038
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Human clinical trial

Tirzepatide versus Semaglutide Once Weekly in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes (SURPASS-2)

Head-to-head phase 3 trial comparing tirzepatide with semaglutide 1 mg in patients with type 2 diabetes (1,879 participants).

Frías JP, et al. New England Journal of Medicine, 2021-08-05. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2107519
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Human clinical trial

Triple-Hormone-Receptor Agonist Retatrutide for Obesity — A Phase 2 Trial

Phase 2 trial of the GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist retatrutide in adults with obesity over 48 weeks (338 participants).

Jastreboff AM, et al. New England Journal of Medicine, 2023-08-10. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2301972
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Human clinical trial

Bremelanotide for the Treatment of Hypoactive Sexual Desire Disorder (RECONNECT)

Two phase 3 trials of the melanocortin receptor agonist bremelanotide in premenopausal women with hypoactive sexual desire disorder (1,202 participants).

Kingsberg SA, et al. Obstetrics & Gynecology, 2019-11. DOI: 10.1097/AOG.0000000000003500
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Review

Regenerative and Protective Actions of the GHK-Cu Peptide in the Light of the New Gene Data

Review of GHK-Cu literature and gene-expression profiling data in human cells.

Pickart L, et al. International Journal of Molecular Sciences, 2018-07-07. DOI: 10.3390/ijms19071987
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Preclinical — in vitro (cell culture)

Epithalon Peptide Induces Telomerase Activity and Telomere Elongation in Human Somatic Cells

Laboratory study of telomerase activity and telomere length in cultured human fibroblast cells exposed to Epithalon. Cell-culture findings; not human clinical data.

Khavinson VK, et al. Bulletin of Experimental Biology and Medicine, 2003-06. DOI: 10.1023/a:1025493705728
Source: Peptide Health Digest
Preclinical — in vitro and animal (mouse)

The Mitochondrial-Derived Peptide MOTS-c Improves Bioenergetics in Aging-Associated Granulosa Cells and Oocytes

Laboratory study of MOTS-c in aged human granulosa cells and oocytes from aged mice, measuring mitochondrial energy production and oocyte maturation. Laboratory and animal findings; not human clinical data.

Tsui KH, et al. Reproduction & Fertility, 2026-10-19. DOI: 10.1530/RAF-26-0161
Source: PubMed
Preclinical — animal (rat)

Retatrutide treatment in streptozotocin-induced diabetic rats: renal inflammatory cytokines, HIF-1α expression, and histopathological injury profile

Rat study measuring kidney inflammatory markers, HIF-1α gene expression and tissue scoring after retatrutide in a streptozotocin diabetes model; reported no statistically confirmed tissue-level difference. Animal findings; not human clinical data.

Keskin U, et al. Journal of Molecular Histology, 2026-10-15. DOI: 10.1007/s10735-026-11013-9
Source: PubMed
Human clinical trial

Retatrutide Once Weekly for Treatment of Obstructive Sleep Apnea

Two phase 3 placebo-controlled trials (TRIUMPH-1-OSA and TRIUMPH-2-OSA) of the investigational GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist retatrutide in adults with moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea and obesity or overweight, with or without type 2 diabetes, with change in the apnea-hypopnea index at 80 weeks as the primary endpoint (691 participants).

Grunstein RR, et al. New England Journal of Medicine, 2026-10-21. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2611477
Source: PubMed
Systematic review

Human evidence for BPC-157 in orthopaedic and regenerative medicine: a systematic review of efficacy and safety

Systematic review of published human studies of BPC-157. Three small studies (30 participants in total) met the inclusion criteria; all were judged to be of poor methodological quality and the overall certainty of evidence was rated very low.

Poolton JT, et al. Acta Orthopaedica Belgica, 2026-10-08. DOI: 10.52628/92.3.15837
Source: PubMed
Cross-sectional study

Self-Reported Adverse Effects Among Social Media Users Taking Nonapproved Retatrutide

Cross-sectional analysis of adverse effects self-reported in social media posts by people describing their own use of nonapproved retatrutide. Self-reported, unverified data; not a clinical trial.

Sehgal NKR, et al. JAMA Network Open, 2026-10-01. DOI: 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.38929
Source: PubMed
Narrative review

Investigational Peptides in Orthopaedic Practice: Evidence, Safety, and Patient Counseling for BPC-157, Thymosin Beta-4, and TB-500

Narrative review of preclinical and human evidence, product-quality concerns, regulatory status and anti-doping status for BPC-157, thymosin beta-4 and products marketed as TB-500. The authors report that human musculoskeletal evidence for BPC-157 is limited to one small uncontrolled case series and that none of the three has an FDA-approved musculoskeletal indication.

Tham A, et al. Journal of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons, 2026-10-27. DOI: 10.5435/JAAOS-D-26-00049
Source: PubMed
Viewpoint

Peptides, FDA's Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee, and the Future of FDA Drug Approval

Viewpoint discussing the FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee's recommendations on adding peptides to the 503A bulks list and the authors' concerns about the effect on the drug-approval process. Opinion article; not a study.

Samantaroy S, et al. JAMA, 2026-10-26. DOI: 10.1001/jama.2026.19125
Source: PubMed
Medical news

Unapproved GLP-1 Drugs Retatrutide and Zenagamtide Sold Online Are No Bargain

JAMA Medical News article on online sales of two unapproved GLP-1 drugs, retatrutide and zenagamtide, and the potential risks of products sold outside approved channels. News article; not a study.

Rubin R. JAMA, 2026-10-09. DOI: 10.1001/jama.2026.20514
Source: PubMed
Case report

Emerging risks of health optimisation: refractory nausea associated with peptide injections reported to contain retatrutide

Single-patient case report from Switzerland of persistent nausea and vomiting after injections of an unverified product reported to contain retatrutide, an investigational drug, supplied in a wellness setting. A single case; not a clinical trial.

Buzzi RM, Huber LC. BMJ Case Reports, 2026-10-07. DOI: 10.1136/bcr-2026-276106
Source: PubMed
Narrative review

Retatrutide as a Unimolecular GIP/GLP-1/Glucagon Receptor Tri-Agonist: Molecular Design, Pharmaceutical Development and Critical Interpretation of Emerging Phase 3 Evidence

Critical narrative review of the molecular design, pharmacokinetics, pharmaceutical development and clinical evidence for the investigational drug retatrutide through 11 September 2026. The authors note that only one phase 3 trial (TRANSCEND-T2D-1) has a full peer-reviewed report and that the obesity-trial results are sponsor topline disclosures that remain provisional.

Mateescu DM, et al. Pharmaceutics, 2026-10-09. DOI: 10.3390/pharmaceutics18101277
Source: PubMed
Narrative review

Possibilities for Cytoprotective Liver Therapy, Stable Gastric Pentadecapeptide BPC 157, and Standard Agents — A Narrative Review

Narrative review of experimental liver-injury models comparing BPC 157 with other agents studied in those models. The evidence reviewed is preclinical; the authors state that it does not establish clinical efficacy and that clinical studies remain necessary.

Sever M, et al. Pharmaceuticals, 2026-10-01. DOI: 10.3390/ph19101559
Source: PubMed
Preclinical — in vitro and animal (mouse)

Mots-c attenuates diabetic nephropathy by downregulating LARS1 to promote lipophagy and suppress tubular senescence

Laboratory study of MOTS-c in db/db diabetic mice and cultured human kidney tubular cells (HK-2), measuring kidney injury, lipid deposition and cellular senescence markers. Laboratory and animal findings; not human clinical data.

Wang J, et al. Biochemical Pharmacology, 2026-10-23. DOI: 10.1016/j.bcp.2026.118572
Source: PubMed
Narrative review

Peptides in Aesthetic Dermatology: Classification, Fibroblast-Centered Mechanisms, Delivery Technologies, and Clinical Evidence

Critical narrative review classifying peptides used in aesthetic dermatology, including copper-binding peptides, and their relationship to dermal fibroblasts. The authors note that many human trials are limited by small samples, short follow-up, industry sponsorship or multi-ingredient formulations.

Yi KH, et al. Life, 2026-10-02. DOI: 10.3390/life16101660
Source: PubMed

Federal Register notice

Enforcement Discretion Policy Regarding 503A Compounding Using Certain Peptide Bulk Drug Substances

HHS notice of availability of a policy under which HHS does not intend to take enforcement action against state-licensed pharmacies, Federal facilities or licensed physicians compounding drugs from the peptide bulk drug substances listed in the policy's Appendix A while the 503A bulks list is developed through rulemaking. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Filed for public inspection 2026-10-28; scheduled for publication 2026-10-30 (Docket HHS-OASH-2026-0496, RIN 0910-ZD94).

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office of the Secretary, Federal Register document 2026-22192, filed 2026-10-28.
