About This Site

Comparing peptide vendors honestly takes more than a price column.

Discount Peptides exists to make that comparison easier — real prices, side by side, from vendors that have actually been checked, not just listed.

What this site does

Research peptides are sold by a large and growing number of independent vendors, and prices for the same compound can vary a lot from one supplier to the next. This site pulls pricing for a given peptide across multiple vendors into one place, so the comparison itself is straightforward. Where possible, pricing updates automatically, straight from each vendor's own product pages — not a number typed in once and forgotten.

How a vendor ends up listed here

Every vendor on this site goes through the same process before anything of theirs gets compared:

  • 01
    Legitimacy checkReal business signals — reviews from independent sources, verifiable contact information, an actual track record — not just a professional-looking storefront.
  • 02
    Legal-risk filtering, product by productPrescription drugs, compounds still in active clinical trials, and a handful of specific identity-risk blends are excluded from the comparison — see below for exactly what and why.
  • 03
    Real pricing, not marketing copyPrices shown are read directly from each vendor's own product pages. Where a vendor's testing or quality claims are shown, they're sourced from the vendor directly, not assumed.

What never gets listed here

Regardless of how a vendor otherwise checks out, these categories are excluded from every comparison on this site:

Prescription drugs sold without a prescription Compounds still in active clinical trials SARMs with an FDA warning history TB-500 and TB-500 blends Melanotan II

Some of these show up under different vendors' own generic or shortened names — the filtering is applied by what a compound actually is, not just what it's labeled.

How this site makes money

Some vendors on this site are affiliate partners — if you buy from one through a link here, this site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That relationship never changes which vendors are listed or excluded, or how prices are compared: the filtering above applies the same way whether or not a vendor has an affiliate arrangement with this site.
