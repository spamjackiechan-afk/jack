Our Values

What we hold ourselves to — and nothing we can’t back up.

Testing, described honestly

For every supplier we record what their published testing actually shows — methods, the named lab if any, public COAs — and note gaps and red flags. We don’t run our own lab tests, and a COA is not a guarantee. Testing & Quality

Prices checked at the source

Our price checker reads suppliers’ own product pages. Each run is timestamped and saved to version history, so changes are on record — not silently overwritten.

Affiliate commissions, disclosed

We earn affiliate commissions from some listed suppliers, at no extra cost to you. It doesn’t change the prices shown, and listings and sort order are not paid placements — suppliers are ordered by price (or by real visitor clicks, if you choose).

Corrections welcome

We read every message and fix errors fast. Wrong price, missing supplier, broken link — tell us.
