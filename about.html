About This Site

On comparison, and what it does not excuse.

There is no shortage of places to buy research peptides, and increasingly little agreement among them — on price, on documentation, on what, precisely, ought to be trusted. Discount Peptides was built to address one narrow part of that problem: comparison, conducted as carefully as a single small operation can reasonably manage, and offered without pretending to be more than what it is.

The premise is simple enough to state plainly. A given compound is often sold, under the same name, by a dozen or more independent suppliers, at prices that can differ by a considerable margin and for reasons rarely disclosed. This site gathers those prices — reading them, where possible, directly and automatically from each vendor's own listings, rather than relying on figures entered once and left to grow stale — and sets them beside one another, so that the comparison itself asks nothing further of the visitor.

No vendor appears here by default, and none is listed on the strength of its own description of itself. Each is examined first for the ordinary signs of a legitimate operation — reviews from sources with no reason to flatter it, contact information that resolves to something real, a history that can be checked rather than taken on faith. Each vendor's catalog is then read product by product, and a defined set of categories is withheld from comparison, regardless of how the vendor otherwise conducts its business.

Withheld, specifically: prescription medications offered without a prescription; compounds that remain under active clinical investigation and have not completed the ordinary course to market; a class of SARMs with a documented history of FDA warning letters; TB-500, and blends built around it, owing to unresolved questions of identity and verification; and Melanotan II, which carries its own explicit warnings against use. Several of these appear, on various vendors' own pages, under abbreviated or generic names — the exclusion is applied to what a compound is, not merely to what it is called there.

A note on how the site is supported, since it ought to be stated rather than left to be inferred: certain listed vendors maintain an affiliate arrangement with this site, under which a purchase made through a listed link may return a commission, at no cost to the purchaser. That arrangement is held apart, deliberately, from the standards described above. A vendor's affiliate status has no bearing on whether it is listed or excluded, or on how its pricing is presented beside others.

Comparison is offered here. Endorsement is not.
